JUNIOR BIBLE QUIZ

Download JrBQ

Android app for phones, tablets and Chromebooks. Version 4.2 gives you two ways to play from the home screen. Play Solo: the app is the quizmaster, so one person can start a session and play on their own, like any quiz app. Each question appears up to the key words, Complete question shows the rest, and you type your answer. Host & Players: the multiplayer bout with a quizmaster, buzzers live from the start of every question, Fill players, the host's choice of whether players see the question, and joining with the game code. Study material is included.
