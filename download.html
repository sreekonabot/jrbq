JUNIOR BIBLE QUIZ

Download JrBQ

Android app for phones, tablets and Chromebooks. Version 4.0 adds Fill players: the host can seat computer players in the empty seats (0 up to the free seats). They buzz fast, but only after a short head start for the people at the table (3 seconds by default), and a person who buzzes first always gets the floor. Buzzers are still live from the start of every question, the question appears at once up to the key words, the host chooses whether players see the question, and joining from anywhere with the game code and Study material are included.
